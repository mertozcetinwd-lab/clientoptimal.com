# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners and teams with routine inbound calls that can follow an approved intake script and have a person available for exceptions or follow-up. The intended audience is general across suitable businesses, not limited to one vertical.

## Product Purpose

Client Optimal is an implementation service for custom AI voice agents. It scopes and builds call intake for a business, then provides ongoing support under terms agreed for that project. The website should help a prospective customer understand the service, its boundaries, and the next step: discussing their call flow.

## Positioning

Implementation and ongoing support, not a self-serve voice-agent software product. The business configures a call flow around each customer's phone system, approved script, data needs, and human follow-up route.

## Operating Context

Client Optimal is operated by Mert Ozcetin as a one-person implementation studio. A prospect first describes their phone setup, routine calls, and who handles exceptions. Project-specific scope, provider, costs, timing, support, and privacy settings must be agreed before any live number is changed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- An agent can disclose that it is AI, ask business-approved intake questions, capture agreed details, and route exceptions to a person when configured.
- Each project's phone provider, integrations, notification destination, and handoff behavior remain to be confirmed.
- Do not imply the agent diagnoses, quotes, books, dispatches, or promises a callback time unless that action is configured and verified for the project.
- Do not state generic pricing, launch timing, provider fees, response times, uptime, recording, retention, or model-use terms as settled. Confirm these per project.
- Do not claim a particular industry is the only fit. Suitability depends on the business's call types and follow-up capacity.
- No voice-agent customer results, testimonials, or client deployments are established.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the existing website palette unchanged during this rebrand.
- The site should feel premium and distinctly redesigned while clearly representing a practical AI voice-agent implementation service.

## Evidence on Hand

- The website has an interactive browser sample using Retell. It is a demonstration, not a customer deployment. Microphone audio is sent to the provider after a visitor starts it.
- No voice-agent testimonials or customer results are established.

## Product Principles

- Make AI disclosure and human fallback clear.
- Scope each call path before live routing.
- Keep claims bounded by configured and verified behavior.
- Agree ongoing support terms project by project.
